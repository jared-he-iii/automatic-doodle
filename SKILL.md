# Thesis Workbench Router

## Purpose
Route work on the ancient-pagoda historical-materials thesis through complementary research skills.

## Default pipeline
source-analysis -> academic-research -> literature-review -> thesis-structure -> manuscript-writing-review -> citation-verification -> critical-thinking -> targeted-re-review

## Gate 1: Evidence
Before drafting a factual historical claim, identify its evidence and limitations.

## Gate 2: Structure
Before polishing prose, ensure the paragraph serves the research question and chapter argument.

## Gate 3: Citation
Before accepting a new reference, verify both bibliographic existence and claim support.

## Gate 4: Critical review
After revision, independently challenge causal links, generalizations, source classification, and teaching claims.

## Output contract
Every major revision should report:
- changed section
- reason for change
- evidence used
- citations added/removed
- unresolved evidence gaps
- remaining critical-thinking risks
